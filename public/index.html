Three curves are hiding today: one easy, one medium, one hard. The arrows on the edges show where a curve leaves the graph. Draw where you think it goes. The moment you lift your pen, your line is marked: green where you are on it, red where you are way off.

    Type of curve:

    The real curve is the black line, with your last go faded behind it. Dashed lines are asymptotes: lines the curve gets close to but never touches.

    That is all three for today. Come back tomorrow for new ones.

    Reading your line

    Spot onWay off

    • The line is thick and green where you are on the curve, and gets thinner and redder the further away you are.
    • ● A dot: a hint. This spot is on the curve. Each hint costs points.
    • Scoring: each curve is out of 1000. Your accuracy sets the score, then every go after the first costs 100 and every hint costs 150.
    • ▲ An arrow on the top or bottom edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
    • → A small arrow on your line: you are well off here, and the curve is in that direction. It does not say how far.
    • A grey bar along the bottom: the curve is somewhere above, and you were nowhere near.
    • Your last go stays on the graph, faded, so you can trace the good parts.
    • You can draw any shape you like, including loops and straight up and down.
    Drawing with a keyboard

    Select the graph with Tab. The arrow keys move the pen. Press Space to put the pen down, move to draw, and press Space again to lift it, which finishes the stroke.
