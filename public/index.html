What the colours mean

  • Green, solid: spot on.
  • Orange, dashed: close.
  • Red, dotted: way off, or there is no curve here at all.
  • ▲ Arrow on an edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
  • Grey bar along the bottom: the curve is somewhere above, and you drew nothing.
Drawing with a keyboard

Select the graph with Tab. The arrow keys move the pen. Press Space to put the pen down, move to draw, and press Space again to lift it. With the eraser chosen, the same keys rub out.
