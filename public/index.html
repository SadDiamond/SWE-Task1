Reading your line

Spot onWay off

  • The line is thick and green where you are on the curve, and gets thinner and redder the further away you are.
  • Red also means there is no curve in that spot at all.
  • ▲ An arrow on the top or bottom edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
  • A grey bar along the bottom: the curve is somewhere above, and you drew nothing.
  • Your last go stays on the graph, faded, so you can trace the good parts.
Drawing with a keyboard

Select the graph with Tab. The arrow keys move the pen. Press Space to put the pen down, move to draw, and press Space again to lift it, which finishes the stroke.
